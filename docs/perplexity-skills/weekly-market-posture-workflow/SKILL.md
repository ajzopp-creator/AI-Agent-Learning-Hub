# Weekly Market Posture Workflow

Use this skill when preparing a weekly review of overall market conditions and trading posture.

Evaluate trend, momentum, breadth, sector leadership, watchlist conditions, and overall risk environment in a consistent repeatable workflow.

Return a structured posture summary that helps guide bias, caution level, focus areas, and next trading actions for the coming week.
