## 🔍 Focus Modes: Essential for Trading Research

Focus modes control WHERE Perplexity searches. Same question + different mode = completely different results.

### The 5 Focus Modes:

### 1. **Web Focus** (Default)
- Searches entire internet
- **Best for**: General trading questions, market news, current events
- **Example**: "What are the latest Fed interest rate decisions and market reactions?"

### 2. **Academic Focus** ⭐ Important for Serious Research
- Searches scholarly databases, peer-reviewed journals, research papers
- Sources: PubMed, Semantic Scholar, academic institutions
- **Best for**: Understanding market theories, backtesting research, economic data
- **Example**: "Recent academic studies on momentum trading effectiveness"

### 3. **Social Focus** 👥 Powerful for Sentiment
- Searches Reddit, forums, social platforms
- **Best for**: Trader sentiment, stock opinions, platform reviews
- **Example**: "What do traders on Reddit think about current market conditions?"
- **Caution**: Verify everything - social media can be wrong or biased

### 4. **Video Focus** 📺 Great for Learning
- Searches YouTube and video platforms
- Includes timestamps for specific sections
- **Best for**: Learning new strategies, platform tutorials, chart analysis
- **Example**: "How to set up risk management in ThinkOrSwim tutorial"

### 5. **Writing Focus** ✍️
- Uses AI for creative and professional writing
- No web search involved
- **Best for**: Trade journal entries, plan documentation, strategy writeups
- **Example**: "Write a trading plan template for swing trading"

### How to Switch Modes:
1. Click "Focus" button before searching
2. Select your mode
3. Ask your question
4. Mode applies to all follow-ups in that thread

**Pro Tip for Traders**: Ask the same question in multiple modes:
- Academic: What does research say?
- Social: What do real traders experience?
- Web: What's the current market data?

## 💡 Trading Research Prompts (Copy & Use)

### Market Analysis Prompts:

**1. Sector Analysis:**
"Analyze current trends in the [sector] sector with recent data, key players, and market outlook for the next quarter"

**2. Stock Comparison:**
"Compare [Stock A] and [Stock B] based on: fundamentals, technical indicators, analyst sentiment, and recent news. Include pros and cons of each"

**3. Economic Indicators:**
"How does [economic indicator] historically affect the stock market? Include specific examples from the last 3 recessions"

**4. Earnings Analysis:**
"Analyze [Company]'s latest earnings report. Include: beat/miss, guidance changes, market reaction, and analyst updates"

### Risk Management Prompts:

**1. Correlation Research:**
"What is the historical correlation between [Asset A] and [Asset B]? How does this change during market downturns?"

**2. Volatility Analysis:**
"Analyze current VIX levels compared to historical averages. What does this suggest about market conditions?"

**3. Risk Events:**
"What major economic events or policy decisions are scheduled for [time period]? How might they impact [sector/stock]?"

### Technical Analysis Prompts:

**1. Pattern Recognition:**
"Describe the technical setup for [stock]. Include: current trend, support/resistance levels, key indicators, and recent volume patterns"

**2. Indicator Research:**
"How effective is the [indicator name] for [type of trading]? Include academic research and real-world backtests"

**3. Strategy Validation:**
"Find research on the effectiveness of [trading strategy]. Include win rates, drawdown data, and optimal market conditions"

### Fundamental Research Prompts:

**1. Company Deep Dive:**
"Create a comprehensive analysis of [Company] including: business model, competitive advantages, financial health, management quality, and growth prospects"

**2. Industry Trends:**
"What are the latest disruptive trends in [industry]? Which companies are positioned to benefit or suffer?"

**3. Valuation Check:**
"Is [Stock] overvalued or undervalued based on: P/E ratio, PEG ratio, price-to-book, and peer comparison?"

### News & Sentiment Prompts:

**1. Latest Developments:**
"What are the latest developments for [Stock] in the past week? Include news, analyst changes, and insider activity"

**2. Sentiment Analysis:**
"What is the current market sentiment for [Stock/Sector]? Include social media sentiment, analyst ratings, and institutional activity"

**3. Controversy Check:**
"What are the main risks or controversies surrounding [Company]? Include regulatory issues, lawsuits, and competitive threats"

### Historical Pattern Prompts:

**1. Seasonal Patterns:**
"Analyze seasonal patterns for [Stock/Sector]. What months historically show strength or weakness?"

**2. Event Studies:**
"How has the market historically reacted to [type of event]? Provide specific examples with dates and percentage moves"

**3. Backtesting Research:**
"Find research on backtesting [strategy name]. What are the optimal parameters and expected performance metrics?"

### Educational Prompts:

**1. Concept Explanation:**
"Explain [trading concept] in simple terms with real examples from actual trades"

**2. Strategy Breakdown:**
"Break down the [trading strategy] step-by-step for someone learning it for the first time. Include entry rules, exit rules, and risk management"

**3. Tool Comparison:**
"Compare [Indicator A] and [Indicator B] for [type of trading]. When should I use each one?"

## ⚠️ Critical Warnings for Traders

### 1. **Perplexity is a RESEARCH tool, NOT a trading signal**

❌ Don't: Take trades based solely on Perplexity answers  
✅ Do: Use it to gather information, then make YOUR decision

### 2. **AI can hallucinate or be wrong**

❌ Don't: Trust numbers without verification  
✅ Do: Always verify critical data (prices, dates, earnings) from official sources

### 3. **Social sentiment can be misleading**

❌ Don't: Follow the herd based on Reddit sentiment  
✅ Do: Use sentiment as ONE data point among many

### 4. **Past performance ≠ Future results**

❌ Don't: Assume historical patterns will repeat exactly  
✅ Do: Use historical research to understand probabilities and context

### 5. **Information is not an edge**

❌ Don't: Think faster research = automatic profits  
✅ Do: Use research to SUPPORT your edge (discipline, risk management, psychology)
