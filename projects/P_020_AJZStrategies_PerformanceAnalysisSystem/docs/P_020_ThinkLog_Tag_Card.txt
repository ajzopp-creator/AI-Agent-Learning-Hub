P_020 THINKLOG TAG CARD
========================
Format (first line of ThinkLog body):
MMDD: [WHY] [SIG] optional free text
Example: 0428: [BTD] [A] tight bounce off 50DMA, RSI 28, 3:1 R:R

WHY -- SYSTEM
  BTD    P_115  Buy The Dip
  OIL    P_116  Options Income Launchpad
  EXT    P_117  External recommendation
  EZB    P_118  Eddie Z Breakouts
  VPT    P_300  VantagePoint
  SNT    P_100  BigTrends / Sunday Night Trader
  DAY    P_200  Intraday, flat by close

WHY -- SITUATION
  ASYM     Near-miss BUY signal, needs review
  IFFY     Setup marginal / didn't fully trigger
  LEARN    Educational / testing a theory
  CROWDED  Everyone else is in this
  FOMO     Be honest with yourself
  REVENGE  Trying to make back a loss -- review brutally

SIG -- SIGNAL STRENGTH
  A   High conviction, all signals aligned
  B   Standard, system fired, no red flags
  C   Marginal, fired but something feels off
  X   Counter-signal, taking it anyway, flag for review

Rule: WHY + SIG both required for the tag to parse.
No brackets = trade imports fine, just untagged (reason/signal_strength = null).
