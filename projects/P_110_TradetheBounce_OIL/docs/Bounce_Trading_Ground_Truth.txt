Paper 1: Evidence and Behaviour of Support and Resistance Levels in Financial Time SeriesSource Platform: ResearchGateAccess Link: ResearchGate Full Text & AbstractAbstract Core:"This paper investigates the phenomenon of support and resistance levels (SR levels) in financial time series, which act as temporary price barriers that reverse price trends. We develop a heuristic discovery algorithm for this purpose, to discover and evaluate SR levels for intraday price series. Our simple approach discovers SR levels which are able to reverse price trends statistically significantly. Asset price entering SR levels with higher number of price bounces before are more likely to bounce on such SR levels again. We also show that the decay aspect of the discovered SR levels as decreasing probability of price bounce over time."  Paper 2: Support for Resistance: Technical Analysis and Intraday Exchange RatesAuthor: Carol Osler (Brandeis University / Federal Reserve Bank of New York)  Access Links:Federal Reserve Bank of NY (Official PDF/Abstract)IDEAS/RePEc Academic RepositoryAbstract Core:"Support and resistance levels—points at which an exchange rate trend may be interrupted and reversed—are widely used for short-term exchange rate forecasting. Nevertheless, the levels' ability to predict intraday trend interruptions has never been rigorously evaluated. This article undertakes such an analysis, using support and resistance levels provided to customers by six firms active in the foreign exchange market. The author offers strong evidence that the levels help to predict intraday trend interruptions."
