## 1. Base Breakout

- Sideways base
- Volume dries up
- Resistance tested
- Breakout with volume

**Rule:** Buy strength, not hope.

## 2. First Pullback

- Strong breakout
- Pullback to 21 EMA
- Bullish reversal candle
- Trend resumes

**Rule:** Let others chase. Buy the pullback.

## 3. VCP Pattern

*(Volatility Contraction Pattern)*

- C1 = -20%
- C2 = -12%
- C3 = -5%
- Volatility contracts
- Supply dries up
- Expansion follows

**Rule:** Smaller pullbacks = bigger opportunity.

## 4. Tightness Pattern

- Strong uptrend
- Base formation
- Very tight range
- Low volume
- Breakout

**Rule:** Tight stocks become fast stocks.

## 5. 5 Star Zone

- Monthly RSI > 60
- Weekly RSI > 60
- Price above 21 EMA
- Daily RSI near 40 or 60
- Bullish price action

**Rule:** Follow strength, not cheap stocks.
