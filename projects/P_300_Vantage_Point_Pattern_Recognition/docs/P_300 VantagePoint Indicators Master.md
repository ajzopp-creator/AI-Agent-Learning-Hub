## 1. Neural Index (NI / NeuralX.Max)

**Purpose:**  
Predicts whether prices are likely to move **UP or DOWN over the next 2 days**.

> “When the Neural Index is green, the market is expected to trend upward over the next two days.”  
> “When the Neural Index is red, the market is expected to trend downward over the next two days.”

### How It Works
- **Green = Bullish (next 48 hours)**
- **Red = Bearish (next 48 hours)**
- NeuralX.Max = normalized strength over last 90 days.

### Key Interpretation
- Focus on **color flips** (Red → Green or Green → Red).
- Best used as **short‑term confirmation** with crossovers & predictive differences.

## 2. Predictive Trading Range (Predicted High / Predicted Low)

**Purpose:**  
Forecasts **tomorrow’s expected high and low**.

### Color Logic
- **Green shading = Bullish**
  - Predicted High > prior Predicted High  
  - Predicted Low > prior Predicted Low  
- **Red shading = Bearish**
  - Predicted High < prior Predicted High  
  - Predicted Low < prior Predicted Low  
- **Mixed = Indecision / Consolidation**

> “Mixed colors = uncertainty… can signal consolidation
