Ruben Hassid argues that most users **don’t need the $100 Claude plan yet**.

## **1. Upload PDFs the right way**  
- Raw PDF = **~3,000 tokens per page**   [Current page](citation-section://89304734/2)  
- Convert to Google Doc → export as `.md` → **<200 tokens**   [Current page](citation-section://89304734/3)  

---

## **2. Don’t build files in Cowork too early**  
- Plan in Chat first   [Current page](citation-section://89304734/4)  
- Move to Cowork only when you know exactly what you want   [Current page](citation-section://89304734/5)  

---

## **3. Stop writing long prompts**  
- People write 500‑word prompts that reload unnecessarily   [Current page](citation-section://89304734/6)  
- Use this 29‑word structure instead:  
  **“I want to [task] to [goal]. Ask me questions using AskUserQuestion.”**   [Current page](citation-section://89304734/7)  

---

## **4. Don’t redo entire outputs**  
- Instead of “redo everything,” say:  
  **“Only redo section 3. Keep everything else. No commentary.”**  
    [Current page](citation-section://89304734/9)  

---

## **5. Batch tasks into one message**  
- Don’t send 3 separate messages for 3 tasks   [Current page](citation-section://89304734/10)  
- Use:  
  **“Summarize this, list the points, suggest a headline.”**   [Current page](citation-section://89304734/12)
