The 21 EMA isn't just a moving average.

It's where many of the best low-risk entries begin. 📈

Most traders chase breakouts.

Experienced traders wait for the breakout to come back to them.

My ideal 21 EMA pullback checklist:

✅ Weekly RSI > 60

✅ Price above 50 & 200 EMA

✅ Pullback into the 21 EMA (dynamic support)

✅ Volume dries up on the pullback

✅ Bullish reversal candle + buying volume returns

That's where probability shifts in your favor.

🚫 Avoid these mistakes:

❌ Buying 8–10% above the 21 EMA

❌ Ignoring volume during pullbacks

❌ Treating the 21 EMA as an exact line instead of a support zone

❌ Forgetting risk management

📌 One lesson that changed my trading:

The market pays you for patience, not for chasing.

The best traders don't buy because price is moving.

They buy because risk is low and the setup is complete.
