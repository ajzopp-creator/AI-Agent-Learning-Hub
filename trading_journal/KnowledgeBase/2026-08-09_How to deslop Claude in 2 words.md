## 1\. The ASD-STE100 magic.

ASD-STE100 is simplified English for instructions.

![ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English [TechScribe technical writing]](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3kRk!,w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0f9152dc-cfe6-4ae5-b6ba-c63f402d074e_891x609.png)

ASD-STE100 is an international standard for writing clear, simple, and safe technical manuals. Super famous standard for aerospace for example.

So when you ask AI to adhere to ASD-STE100, it works.

AI knows enough about the ASD-STE100 to reply with simplified English.

Now we could go the extra mile and build a Claude skill with the rigorous ASD-STE100 list. But who is crazy enough to build such skill?
