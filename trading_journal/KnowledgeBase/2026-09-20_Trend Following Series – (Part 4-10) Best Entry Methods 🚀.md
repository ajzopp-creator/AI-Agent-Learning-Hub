📈 Trend Following Series – (Part 4/10): Best Entry Methods 🚀

A good trend is useless without the right entry! 🎯

Here are 4 high-probability ways to enter a trend:

1️⃣ Pullback Entry 📉➡️📈

✅ Wait for price to retrace to support/50 EMA

✅ Enter when trend resumes

⭐ Safer than chasing moves

2️⃣ Breakout Entry 🚀

✅ Find consolidation zones

✅ Enter after strong resistance breakout

⚠️ Avoid entering after a huge candle

3️⃣ Retest Entry 🔄

✅ Let breakout happen first

✅ Wait for price to retest the level

✅ Enter after confirmation

4️⃣ Trendline Bounce 📊

✅ Use higher lows & trendline support

✅ Enter near structure

✅ Confirmation improves quality

🔥 Golden Rules of Trend Following:

✔ Trade with structure

✔ Prefer pullbacks

✔ Wait for confirmation

✔ Always define your risk

✔ Never chase candles

📚 Better Entries = Better Trades 💡
