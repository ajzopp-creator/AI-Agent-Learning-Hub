They read it off one free NSE page in under a minute.

You have the same page. You just never open it.

Here's how to read it 👇

---

First, understand why this matters.

Money doesn't hit one stock at random. It rotates into a whole sector first, then lifts the strongest names inside it.

Find the sector big money is moving into, and you're hunting in the right pond before the crowd shows up.

Here's the path.

---

Step 1

Go to the NSE site. Open the menu, then Market Data, then Indices.

---

Step 2

Click into the sectoral indices view

---

Step 3 - Turn the live stream on.

Now just watch.

One column tells you which sector is being bought hardest today and which is being dumped.

Green stacking up in one group is big money stepping in. Red bleeding in another is big money stepping out.

That's the read.

---

You are not guessing which sector is hot. The exchange is showing you, for free, in real time.

Then you do one thing.

You go hunt your setups only inside the strongest sector, and you leave the weak ones alone.
