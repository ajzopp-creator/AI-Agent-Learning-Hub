📈 Trend Following Trading Series (Part 1/10) 🚀

✨ What is Trend Following?

Trend Following means:

➡️ Identify the market direction

➡️ Trade in the same direction as the trend 📊

📈 In an Uptrend:

✅ Higher Highs

✅ Higher Lows

✅ Enter with trend confirmation

✅ Let the trend continue

✅ Exit with a proper plan 🎯

🔥 Key Rules:

1️⃣ Trade with the Trend, not against it

2️⃣ Use simple tools like EMA & Trendlines

3️⃣ Be patient — wait for the right setup ⏳

4️⃣ Manage risk with a plan 🛡️

💡 “The trend is your friend. Trade with it, not against it.” 📚
