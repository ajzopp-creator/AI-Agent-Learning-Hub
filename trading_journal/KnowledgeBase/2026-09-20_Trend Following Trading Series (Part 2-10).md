📊 Trend Following Trading Series (Part 2/10) 🚀

🔍 How to Identify a Trend?

Before entering any trade, first understand the market direction 📈📉

There are 3 types of trends:

🟢 1. Uptrend (Bullish Trend)

✅ Higher Highs (HH)

✅ Higher Lows (HL)

✅ Price above moving averages

✅ Look for buying opportunities 📈

🔴 2. Downtrend (Bearish Trend) ✅ Lower Highs (LH)

✅ Lower Lows (LL)

✅ Price below moving averages

✅ Look for selling opportunities 📉

🟡 3. Sideways Market (Range Bound)

➡️ No clear direction

➡️ Price moves between Support & Resistance

➡️ Wait for breakout confirmation ⏳

🔥 Key Rules:

1️⃣ Identify the trend before trading

2️⃣ Use price structure + moving averages

3️⃣ Avoid trend-following trades in sideways markets

4️⃣ Stay disciplined and follow the trend 🎯

💡 Right Trend = Right Opportunities = Better Trading Decisions 📚📈
