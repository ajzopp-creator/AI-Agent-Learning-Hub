📈 Trend Following Trading Series – (Part 3/10) 🚀

Using Moving Averages for Better Entries & Exits 📊

Moving Averages help traders: ✅ Identify the market trend 🔍

✅ Filter noise 📉

✅ Find better entry points 🎯

📌 Simple Rules:

🟢 Price above 50 EMA & 200 EMA = Uptrend 📈

🔴 Price below 50 EMA & 200 EMA = Downtrend 📉

⚡ 50 EMA + 200 EMA Crossover = Trend Confirmation

💡 Trade with the trend, not against it.

🎯 Use pullbacks for better entries.

⏳ Stay in the trade until the trend breaks.

Remember:

📊 Simple tools + proper discipline = better trading decisions.
