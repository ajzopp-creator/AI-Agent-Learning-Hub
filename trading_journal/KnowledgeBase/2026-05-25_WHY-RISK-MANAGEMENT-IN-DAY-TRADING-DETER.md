The email emphasizes that successful day trading hinges on effective risk management, not finding the best setups. Most retail traders fail due to poor risk control, often risking too much per trade and experiencing rapid capital depletion. By limiting position sizes (e.g., 1% of account per trade), traders can avoid significant drawdowns, maintain consistency, and build a sustainable career through small, steady gains rather than chasing high-risk, volatile returns.
