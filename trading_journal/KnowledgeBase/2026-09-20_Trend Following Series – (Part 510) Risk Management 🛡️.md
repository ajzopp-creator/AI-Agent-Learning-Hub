📊 Trend Following Series – (Part 5/10): Risk Management 🛡️

A great setup means nothing without proper risk control.

Survival comes before profits! 🚀

Here are the key rules every trend follower should follow:

1️⃣ Place Stop Loss Below Structure 📉

✅ Use swing lows & key levels

✅ Give your trade room to breathe

❌ Avoid random tight stops

2️⃣ Avoid Oversized Risk ⚠️

✅ Don’t chase extended moves

✅ Big risks can destroy consistency

3️⃣ Position Sizing Matters 📏

✅ Fix your risk per trade

✅ Adjust position size based on stop distance

4️⃣ Plan Reward Before Entry 🎯

✅ Look for good Risk:Reward setups

✅ Aim for at least 1:2 R:R

✅ Let winners run, cut losers quickly

🔥 Golden Rules:

🛡️ Protect your capital

📌 Trade with structure

💰 Control your position size

🎯 Demand quality R:R

⚡ Stay consistent

Small Risk + Discipline = Long-Term Growth 📈
