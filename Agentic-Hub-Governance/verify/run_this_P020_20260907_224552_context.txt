before writing ("go"):
  1. Multi-leg combo orders (IRON CONDOR, VERTICAL, etc.) collapse to
     their primary leg only -- continuation leg rows (no Order ID of
     their own) are skipped, not modeled as separate legs. Same
     simplification order_linkage.py already makes for Schwab spreads.
  2. "RE #<id>" (order replaced/modified) rows are skipped, not
     chain-followed to the order they replaced.
  3. No distinct fill timestamp exists in the Order History section
     (only "Time Placed") -- closeTime is set equal to enteredTime as a
     best-effort approximation. Also: MKT orders show '~' instead of a
     price in this section (real fill price lives in the separate

     correctly comes back None for these rather than a fabricated
     number, since order_reconciler.py already handles a None fill
     price gracefully (this behavior is directly exercised by check 6
     below, not just asserted).
