## WHY

Tony wanted Cash Available for Trading refreshed automatically on the same
twice-daily cadence P_010 already uses for risk posture (9:30 AM + 2:00 PM),
instead of the prior weekly/on-demand Schwab pull -- while keeping the
ability to override cash on a specific trade. Separately, P_000's Account
Parameters file had accumulated a "Critical Rules" section (cash-per-trade,
three-gate formula, options management/display rules) that duplicated
content already canonical in P_400's own docs, and listed P_115 under
"Applies To" for sizing rules P_115 itself doesn't execute (P_400 sizes
P_115 trades on its behalf, per p115-project-context skill and
P_400_TradeordermanagementGuidelines_v1.1.md).

## WHAT WAS BUILT

**P_400 (code):**
1. `python\schemas.py` -- `AccountParams` gained `cash_available: Optional[float] = None`. Additive, backward-compatible.
