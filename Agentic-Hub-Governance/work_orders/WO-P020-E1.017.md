## WHY

Discovered 2026-09-05 investigating a P_210 (new vertical-credit-spread
subscription) live trade on NDXP. Tony executed a 2-leg bear-call credit
spread as a single combo order (short NDXP 29520C + long NDXP 29530C, same
Schwab `orderId`), then rolled the long leg as a second combo order (close
29530C + open NDXP 29540C, same `orderId`).

`_aggregate_by_order()` in `schwab_mapper.py` groups fills by `order_id`
only, then sums qty and takes a weighted-average price across the whole
group. That's correct for genuine same-contract partial fills (e.g. QBTS
2+2 contracts, same strike, same order). It's wrong for combo orders,
where a single `order_id` covers two *different* option contracts
(different strikes, opposite directions/positionEffect) -- Schwab assigns
one order_id to the whole multi-leg order, not one per leg.

Result: the two real legs of the entry order (short $4.50 / long $3.55)
got averaged into one fabricated "long call, qty 2, entry $4.025" position.
The roll's close+open (close $5.15 / open $4.05) got averaged into an
equally fake "exit $4.60, +$115" print. Neither figure corresponds to any
real transaction -- the true result was a 20-point-wide credit spread
(short 29520C / long 29540C, $202.36 net credit collected) that appears to
have settled near max loss (~$1,797.64) given NDX closed well above both
strikes and account value fell ~$1,958 over the same period.

## FIX

Change `_aggregate_by_order()`'s grouping key from `fill["order_id"]` alone
to `(fill["order_id"], fill["full_symbol"])`. `full_symbol` already carries
the full OCC-style contract identifier including strike (e.g.
`NDXP  260904C29530000`, captured at `_parse_transaction()` line ~185) --
it is populated today but never used as part of the aggregation key. This
keeps genuine multi-fill aggregation working (same contract, same order,
split fills) while correctly splitting combo/spread orders into separate
per-leg rows.
