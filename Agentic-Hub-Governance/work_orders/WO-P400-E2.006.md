## WHY

The three-gate concentration cap ($1,633 max position) limits high-priced stocks
to 1 share (e.g. EME at $823 = 1 share max). Tony correctly overrides to 2 shares
based on trader judgment, but the spec output reflects 1 share and the paper record
must be corrected manually. This creates audit drift between spec output and actual
order.

## WHAT

Add `--qty-override INT` flag to `evaluate` and `spec` CLI commands.

When provided:
- Spec renders with the overridden qty
- EvaluationResult carries the override qty
- Dollar risk recalculated at override qty * risk_per_share
- A visible annotation printed: "[QTY OVERRIDE: N shares -- concentration gate bypassed by trader]"

The override does NOT bypass Council gates. APPROVED verdict is required first.

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

1. `cli.py spec EME --snapshot ... --cash ... --qty-override 2` renders 2-share spec
2. Dollar risk in spec reflects 2 shares
3. Override annotation visible in output
4. Paper record written with correct qty when P_800 write called

- `python/application/build_order_spec.py` ? accept qty_override param
