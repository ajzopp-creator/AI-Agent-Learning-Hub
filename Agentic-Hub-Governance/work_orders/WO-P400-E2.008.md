## WHY

When a Tier 2A dossier results in no trade (entry missed, RR invalid, manual pass),
there is no record that the signal was reviewed. BLOCKED records exist for Council
kills; no equivalent existed for pre-Council drops. Without capture, signal review
history is lost.

- Add drop_reason field to P400Record in ault_schemas.py
- Valid values: ENTRY_MISSED | RR_INVALID | MANUAL_PASS | COUNCIL_BLOCK
- council_verdict = "REVIEWED_NO_TRADE" and lifecycle_status = "DROPPED" are
  the disposition markers (reuse existing optional string fields — no new enum enforcement)
- INIT prompt STEP 3A updated with write block for drop disposition
