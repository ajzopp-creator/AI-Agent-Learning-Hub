## WHY

`audit-book` (live Schwab reconciliation, WO-P400-E4.00x) surfaced four
AJZ broker positions with no corresponding P_400 book record: CSX
(260918C00052500, qty 6), CVNA (260918P00078000, qty 1), DINO
(260918C00092500, qty 1), QQQ (qty 5.0185 shares). A DAL options position
(260918C00100000, qty 4) also surfaced the same way -- separate from
today's fresh DAL stock signal, which Tony skipped for this reason.

Tony clarified 2026-08-14: these are not P_400-managed trades gone stale
in the book -- they were never P_400 trades at all. They come from paid
subscription/alert services, entered directly, outside P_115/P_300 signal
generation and outside P_400 evaluate/spec/record. Tony currently tracks
them by hand in an external spreadsheet. Because P_400's book has no
concept of "known external position," every `audit-book` run re-flags
them as untracked/stale noise, indistinguishable from an actual P_400
book data-integrity problem (see also: DLO SUBMITTED-vs-broker mismatch,
same audit run, separate issue -- not in this WO's scope).

## Scope (to be refined at build)

- Decide where these positions should live: a lightweight external-source
  tag in the P_400 book (lifecycle_status value or separate field) vs.
  staying purely in Tony's spreadsheet with `audit-book` simply
  suppressing known-external symbols from its mismatch report.
- If tagged in-book: define minimum fields needed (symbol, vehicle,
  qty, source label) -- explicitly NOT full P_400 lifecycle (no
  evaluate/spec/council verdict -- these were never screened by P_400).
- `audit-book` output: stop presenting tagged external positions as
  "untracked" -- list them separately (e.g. "Known external, not
  P_400-managed") so real book staleness stays visible.
- Reconcile current four (CSX, CVNA, DINO, QQQ) plus the DAL options
  position under whatever mechanism is decided.
- Confirm with Tony whether P_020 should pull these into performance
  reporting or explicitly exclude them (Affects: P_020).

## Out of Scope

- DLO SUBMITTED-vs-broker mismatch -- separate data-hygiene issue,
  needs its own manual correction, not folded into this WO.
- No new evaluate/council logic -- these positions are explicitly
  outside P_400's signal-evaluation authority.
