---

## WHY

Tony runs paper trades to test new theories without risking real capital.
Paper and real records must be kept separate for clean Obsidian analysis.

---

## DESIGN DECISIONS

1. **Where the flag lives:** Final P400 record only. No upstream changes to
   SignalV2 packets or snapshot dicts.
2. **Sizing:** Identical to real -- all three gates apply with live posture
   mode. No shortcuts for paper.
3. **Submission step:** PAPER trades skip Schwab submission. Status written
   as `PAPER` instead of `SUBMITTED`.
4. **Record routing:** `trading_journal\TradeOrderManagement\P400\paper\`
   (separate from `P400\` real-trade book) for clean Obsidian analysis.
5. **Scope:** Per-trade flag (--paper) or session-level flag (--paper-session).
   Per-trade wins when both are set.
