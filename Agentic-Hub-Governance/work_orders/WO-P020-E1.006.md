## WHY

P_020_Trade_Manager.py is 341 lines, over the 300-line hard limit
(python-project-architecture skill). Root cause: cmd_import, cmd_balance,
and cmd_positions contain real orchestration logic (Schwab pulls, DB wipe
on date override, pull-file resolution, account-id mapping) directly in
what should be a thin CLI dispatch file.

Surfaced 2026-07-12 while adding a small feature (dashboard wiring +
last_run timestamp) that would have pushed the file further over limit.

## FIX

Move business logic out of cli.py into application/ layer:
- application/import_command.py -- run_import_command() + pull-file /
  account-id helpers (moved from cmd_import, _find_latest_pull,
  _resolve_account_id)
- application/account_commands.py -- run_balance_command() +
  run_positions_command() (moved from cmd_balance, cmd_positions)

P_020_Trade_Manager.py becomes thin: argparse + dispatch + print-only
wrappers calling the above. Target: under 200 lines.

No behavior change intended -- same commands, same flags, same output.
